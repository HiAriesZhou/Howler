## What's Changed

* Capture a region with ⌥⇧H to start an explanation immediately, with a separate configurable shortcut.
* Submit follow-up questions while the model thinks or writes; answers appear in order, with retry and skip controls when paused.
* Choose a preferred reply language in Settings, including following your system language for foreign-language screenshots.
* Clicking outside keeps the letter open, ⌥H conceals or recalls it, and Esc or the corner flame burns and clears it.
