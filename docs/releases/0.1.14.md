## What's Changed

* Capture a region with ⌥⇧H to start an explanation immediately, with a separate configurable shortcut.
* Submit follow-up questions while the model thinks or writes; answers appear in order, with retry and skip controls when paused.
* Choose a preferred reply language in Settings, including following your system language for foreign-language screenshots.
* Clicking outside keeps the letter open, ⌥H conceals or recalls it, and Esc or the corner flame burns and clears it.
* Settings now has General, Prompt and Shortcut tabs, with configurable shortcuts for opening the letter, opening Settings and capturing a region. The flame sits at the bottom-right; menu labels are aligned, complete and free of icons.
* Clear any shortcut to disable it, without a separate switch. Empty shortcuts stay disabled after restart; menu actions remain available. Recording and restoring defaults check for conflicts.
* Add or replace your API key directly in Settings, then save and check the connection. Keychain failures now keep their diagnostic details and preserve existing keys; setup guidance stops repeated imports, and connection checks have a 30-second deadline.
